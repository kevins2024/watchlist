Ranks are each team's poll position entering that week. Records reflect completed games only, tallied up through the week before the one you're viewing — for weeks that haven't happened yet, both simply stay blank rather than showing a placeholder. ★ stars are a watchability guess built from rankings, broadcast slot, records, and the pre-game point spread when one's posted (tight lines count for, blowout lines count against) — never real viewership data, since no free source for that exists. Once a game is final, a close finish or an upset quietly pulls its score back up even if the line said blowout — the spread and the final margin are used for that math but never shown. 🔥 Nail-biter means the score was within 16 at the end of the 3rd quarter and didn't blow open wider than that by the final whistle — not just a close final number, which a heavy favorite can rack up in garbage time despite a game that was never really in doubt. Falls back to the pregame line when quarter-by-quarter data isn't available. An outright upset always counts, regardless of margin. ♥ follows a team — a strong boost toward their games, but not an automatic 3-star override; other factors still count. 📑 pins a game to your own Watchlist tab, grouped by week, regardless of its score. ☐/☑ marks a game as already watched (dims the row) — separate from the watchlist, so pinning and watched-tracking don't interfere with each other. Every team also has its own small 👁 next to its name — that's the only thing gating its rank, its record, and whether its name highlights as a winner, since a record climbing (or a highlighted name) after you step forward a week gives away who won. It's there for every team, ranked or not, played or not, so the icon itself never hints at anything. ★ stars always show, but the badges behind them (Marquee, Close spread, Following, and especially 🔥 Nail-biter, which gives away that a finished game was close) stay hidden until you tap the row-level 👁 instead — that reveal is per-row and never remembered, so every visit starts hidden again. Kickoff order / Watchability sorts whatever week you're looking at either way. Stepping through weeks moves CFB and NFL together — each resolves the same number of weeks from its own current week, so switching tabs mid-browse lands on the correlated point in both seasons. Everything you mark is saved locally and stays put across visits. Data via ESPN's public scoreboard endpoints.

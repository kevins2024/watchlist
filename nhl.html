Pages by day rather than by week — the NHL plays most nights of the season, so there's no natural weekly round the way CFB/NFL have. Stepping ‹ / › moves to the next or previous date that actually has a game, skipping empty days automatically. Records are plain W-L-OTL and points (2 per win, 1 per overtime/shootout loss), computed from each team's own full-season results strictly before the game you're viewing — not from ESPN's own "record" field, which already includes the game it's attached to and would give away the result the moment you looked. Records are hidden behind each team's own 👁 the same way as the other boards. ★ stars use broadcast slot, a small playoff bump, how close the two teams are in the standings so far, and a close final score or a result against the run of form — a one-goal final, or any game that reached overtime or a shootout, counts as close. 📑 / ☐☑ / ♥ work the same as the other boards, but are kept on their own separate list — not merged into the shared Watchlist page. Schedule and record data are fetched fresh every visit rather than cached, since games in progress or not yet played can't be frozen into local storage without going stale. Data via ESPN's public scoreboard and team-schedule endpoints.

National-team competitions, separate from the club ⚽ Soccer board — World Cup, Euros, Copa América, Nations League, qualifying campaigns by confederation, and friendlies. Windows are clustered from the real fixture list the same way club matchdays are — grouped by the natural gap between kickoffs — since these competitions don't run on a fixed weekly schedule. Some tabs will be empty most of the time (a tournament that only happens every 2–4 years has long gaps between windows, and stays empty here until it's actually active) — that's expected, not an error. Schedule data is fetched fresh every visit rather than cached, since these calendars keep filling in over time. Records are W-D-L and points, tallied from completed windows before the one you're viewing within that same competition, hidden behind each team's own 👁 the same way as the other boards. Before kickoff, the n/100 score is a hype guess: tournament prestige (World Cup, Euros, Copa América), broadcast slot, and how close the two teams are in this competition's own table — thrown out entirely once the match is live (no rating shows at all while it's in progress) or final. The final score is a separate calculation built mostly off goal difference (a draw scores highest, dropping fast from there), plus a flat +/-30 for a followed team winning or losing (a draw is neutral, not a loss), and smaller nudges for an even group-table battle or a confirmed result against the run of form. 📑 / ☐☑ / ♥ work the same as the other boards, but are kept on their own separate list — not merged into the shared Watchlist page. Data via ESPN's public scoreboard endpoints.
